# 🚚 ROBUST-RAG BOT

### Reinventing Last-Mile Delivery with Autonomous Intelligence

Imagine a delivery system where autonomous bots collaborate with delivery vans, learn from their environment, and make intelligent decisions in real time.

ROBUST-RAG BOT is a Reinforcement Learning-powered autonomous delivery framework designed to tackle one of modern logistics' biggest challenges: **the Last-Mile Delivery Problem**.

---

## 🌍 Why This Matters

More than 50% of delivery costs are incurred during the final stage of shipping.

With increasing urban congestion, fuel consumption, labor shortages, and sustainability concerns, traditional delivery models are becoming increasingly inefficient.

This project explores how AI-driven autonomous agents can transform urban logistics into a smarter, greener, and more scalable ecosystem.

---

## 📌 Problem Statement

The last mile of delivery accounts for a significant portion of logistics costs and faces challenges such as:

- Traffic congestion
- Rising fuel costs
- Labor shortages
- Environmental impact
- Growing demand for faster deliveries

Traditional delivery systems are becoming increasingly difficult to scale efficiently.

---

## 💡 Proposed Solution

ROBUST-RAG BOT introduces a cooperative delivery framework where:

1. A delivery van transports packages to a target delivery zone.
2. An autonomous delivery bot is deployed from the van.
3. The bot navigates independently using Reinforcement Learning.
4. Packages are delivered efficiently to customers.

This approach combines intelligent decision-making with sustainable logistics practices.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- Python
- Unity 3D
- ML-Agents Toolkit
- Reinforcement Learning
- PPO (Proximal Policy Optimization)

---

## 🔄 Workflow

Van ➜ Autonomous Bot ➜ Navigation ➜ Delivery ➜ Customer

The AI agent learns through rewards and penalties, allowing it to continuously improve its navigation and delivery strategies.
